About SkyBlue Airlines: Expect Excellence
SkyBlue Airlines is a premier private airline, proudly headquartered on the 12th floor of the Islamabad Stock Exchange (ISE) Towers in Islamabad, Pakistan. As Pakistan's second-largest airline, we are continually expanding our share of the domestic market while setting new benchmarks for air travel quality and affordability.

Our Vision and Leadership
SkyBlue embodies a new era of air travel, guided by a leadership team with decades of invaluable industry experience. Our current shareholding structure includes a distinguished group of investors, notably Ms. Mehrosh, who holds a majority stake. The company is led by Ms. Fiza, who serves with distinction as both the CEO and Chairman of the Board. Our core management team also includes key executives such as Ms. Warda and Ms. Amna, whose expertise drives our commitment to operational excellence.

Fleet and Network
Our modern fleet comprises next-generation Airbus A320 and A321 neo aircraft, ensuring a comfortable, safe, and efficient flying experience for all our passengers. We operate scheduled flights connecting major domestic hubs—Islamabad, Lahore, Karachi, Peshawar, and Multan—with key international destinations, including UAE airports (Dubai, Sharjah, and Abu Dhabi) and KSA airports (Jeddah and Riyadh).

Innovation and Convenience
SkyBlue has seamlessly integrated unique innovations to guarantee both security and affordability for our customers. We were pioneers in the Pakistani aviation sector, introducing several modern technologies to simplify your booking experience. These innovations include complete online reservation systems, online reservation hold with the convenience of in-person payment at various locations, and electronic document handling. SkyBlue was the first airline in Pakistan to introduce modern services like e-ticketing and self check-in kiosk facilities. At SkyBlue, we are dedicated to delivering not just a flight, but an exceptional experience.
